# 🎓 Student's Friend - Ultimate AI Dashboard

Welcome to **Student's Friend**! This isn't just a basic tracker; it's a complete local dashboard built to manage a student's daily hustle. Whether you are tracking assignments or just jotting down random ideas, this app has you covered. 

The highlight? An integrated AI assistant named **Veyon** who acts as your personal secretary!

---

## ✨ Features & Benefits (Kyun use karein?)

*   **🤖 Veyon AI Secretary**: Fully integrated chat system. Don't want to fill out forms? Just tell Veyon: *"I spent 500 on junk food today"* or *"Add a high priority task for my college project"*, and Veyon will automatically parse the data and update your dashboard!
*   **💰 Finance & Goal Tracker**: Log daily expenses by category. Track every rupee against your custom **Study Fund Goal**. Features a live updating pie-chart to visualize where your money is draining.
*   **📝 Smart Task Manager**: Add tasks with deadlines and priority levels (High/Med/Low). Overdue tasks automatically highlight in RED to keep you on your toes.
*   **🗒️ Quick Sticky Notes**: A 3-grid scratchpad for instant notes. Stays saved even when you close the app.
*   **📊 CSV Exports**: One-click download of your tasks and financial records into Excel-friendly formats.
